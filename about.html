Hello! I'm Nicky, and I take photos of people.

Ever since I was young I've been facinated with photography. My first camera was a cheap Kodak point-and-shoot my grandma gave to me, and ever since I first clicked the shutter button I became obsessed with the ability to recreate life and create art with a camera.

I learned how to use a DSLR camera in 7th grade, and have been honing my skills ever since. Take a look at my work in my gallery, and contact me if you're interested in learning more or booking a session!
